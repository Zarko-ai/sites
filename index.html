Strategies & tools
Tips & Resources
Specific, proven techniques and free tools for each of your five goals.
🎧
English — Listening
BBC, TED Talks, podcasts
Best resource: BBC 6 Minute English. Short, structured, transcripts available. Listen once, read the transcript, then listen again.
Shadowing technique: Play a sentence, pause, say it back with the same rhythm and tone. 20 minutes daily of this beats 2 hours of passive listening.
Speed trick: Start at 0.8× speed. Move to 1× by week 3. Try TED Talks for Curious Minds and The English We Speak (BBC).
✍️
English — Writing & Speaking
Journal, Grammarly, self-recording
Daily journal: Write 200 words every day on any topic. Use Grammarly (free version) to check. Review your top 3 mistakes each Friday.
Speaking practice: Record yourself for 2 minutes on a topic. Listen back. Notice pronunciation and grammar. Improve the next day.
Double goal trick: Write and present your YouTube scripts in English — one activity, two goals at once.
🤖
Machine Learning
fast.ai, Andrew Ng, Google Colab
Weeks 1–4: Math foundations + Python. Khan Academy for linear algebra and statistics. fast.ai Part 1 for practical, code-first ML learning.
Weeks 5–8: Andrew Ng's ML Specialization on Coursera — free to audit. Covers regression, classification, and neural networks step by step.
Daily coding: One small exercise in Google Colab (free). Doing beats reading — always. Code every single day, even 30 minutes counts.
For your master's: Keep a learning log. Write each new concept in your own words. This becomes your study material for applications later.
⌨️
Speed Writing & Typing
keybr.com, typing.com
Best tool: keybr.com — adapts to your weakest keys automatically. 20 focused minutes per day is enough. Do not do more; quality beats quantity.
Targets: Baseline → 30 WPM (week 2) → 40 WPM (week 4) → 48 WPM (week 6) → 55+ WPM (week 8). Test every Friday.
Most important rule: Do not look at your hands. This single habit separates slow and fast typists. Trust your muscle memory.
🍽️
YouTube — Halal Food Channel
CapCut, Canva, YouTube Shorts
Weeks 1–2: Research 10 video ideas. Choose a channel name. Create account and logo using Canva (completely free and beautiful).
Weeks 3–4: Film first 2 videos with your phone. Edit with CapCut (free and very easy). Use natural window light — it looks great.
Weeks 5–8: Publish 1 video per week. YouTube Shorts (60–90 seconds) grow the fastest. Add captions — they double your views.
Video ideas to start: "5 easy halal dinners in 30 minutes" · "Halal swaps for popular recipes" · "Street food made halal at home".
⚡
Staying on Track
Consistency always beats perfection
Friday review ritual: Every Friday, spend 30 minutes reviewing the week. What went well? What needs fixing? Set 3 clear intentions for next week.
Breaks are non-negotiable: The 15-minute breaks in your schedule are mandatory. Your focus will be noticeably better after a real break.
Missed a day? Never try to catch up by doubling work. Just continue normally the next day. Consistency over perfection — always.
Same environment: Same chair, same time, same desk every day. Your brain learns to focus faster when the setting is consistent.
